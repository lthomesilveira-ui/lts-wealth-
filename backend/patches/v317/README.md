# Consistent business natures and conflicting bank metadata

Four combinations of restaurant MCCs and contradictory provider categories can
no longer produce an unsupported restaurant guess. An explicit historical
category or strong evidence for the same purchase retains its precedence. The
existing suggestion function is replaced under a complete definition lease;
OID, owner, ACL and all other catalog attributes remain exact.

The private, source/hash-bound nature catalog covers identified clothing,
confectionery, pharmacy, fuel, rental, insurance, delivery, hotel, fitness and
airline merchants. It requires exact merchant patterns, MCCs, provider categories
and active canonical categories. An existing proposal must agree. Matching
historical preferences are accepted, including health rather than pharmacy for
a pharmacy source whose actual history uses health. Existing choices are never
overwritten, and ambiguous category pairs are not merged.

Only explicitly listed fitness and airline identities can fill an absent
generic activity mapping. They must have their exact expected MCC and provider
activity, no conflicting history and no transfer or other funding operation.
Carrier MCCs 3248 and 3300 are documented in the official October 2026 Visa
Merchant Data Standards Manual. A generic airline, a different carrier code,
an intermediary or incomplete evidence is insufficient.

Every audit attributes the action to the assistant under the owner's
authorization and explicitly disclaims personal confirmation, specific products,
clinical or personal purposes, beneficiary, property, policy identity, coverage
and recurrence inference. Only a broad spending category is applied. There is
no financial-event creation, bank-source mutation, new client endpoint or
published UI change. Decisions continue to inform review suggestions.

PostgreSQL 17 exercises the actual V312/V315/V316 engines and the new rules,
with synthetic data only. It covers coherent and conflicting metadata,
historical precedence, exact carrier identity, missing mappings, funding,
existing choices, hash guards, role denial, parity and idempotence. The browser
consumes those native payloads through the immutable V312 assets at three widths.

Historical POSTED and PENDING sources may receive a nature. Their exact bank
status is recorded in evidence and never rewritten. Classification of a pending
source does not assert payment or realization. Current and future reference
months remain excluded, including otherwise matching pending transactions.
