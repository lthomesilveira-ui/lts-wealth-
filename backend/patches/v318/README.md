# Animal-care spending nature

An active canonical animal-care category allows historical sources from
identified pet retailers, veterinary services, a veterinary-plan business and
an identified animal-care business to receive a broad spending nature.
The merchant pattern, exact MCC, provider activity and original source/hash
must agree with a bounded catalog of primary business references.

The category describes products or services for animals. It does not identify
an animal, person, purchased item, specific service, insurance policy, plan,
coverage, renewal or recurrence. Original bank status is recorded and preserved;
classifying a pending source does not assert realization or payment.

A specific veterinary-plan business may replace the unconfirmed bank-derived
insurance guess. An explicit historical insurance preference, or any existing
category, person or property choice, is preserved. Generic payment
intermediaries, different MCCs, unknown merchant identities and current/future
reference months remain excluded.

This patch introduces private invoker helpers only. Category registration and
source-specific application are separate audited, owner-scoped operations.
No existing category, expense, bank source, amount or published release changes.
PostgreSQL 17 and immutable V312 browser checks use synthetic sources, verify
learning and the new option, and cover explicit choices, wrong codes, role and
hash guards, parity and replay. No production data is committed or used in CI.
