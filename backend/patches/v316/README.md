# Verified spending natures, separate from insurance contracts

Historical card sources from four identified businesses can receive a broad
spending category when their exact merchant pattern, bank MCC, raw provider
category, active canonical category and current review suggestion agree.
The businesses are Evino (beverages), 5àsec (laundry), Bradesco Vida and MAPFRE
(insurance). Primary business references are carried by each candidate.

An insurance charge's broad nature does not identify its policy, insured person,
property, beneficiary, payment schedule or current coverage. The audit explicitly
records these limits and identifies the assistant acting under the owner's
authorization; it does not claim the owner personally confirmed a category.

Only an exact source/hash manifest can be applied. Current or future cycles,
deleted, non-BRL, non-posted or credit sources, incomplete or conflicting
evidence, retired categories and explicit existing choices are excluded. Replay
is idempotent. The functions are private invoker functions; no client endpoint,
new expense, broad semantic rule or bank-source mutation is introduced.

Native PostgreSQL 17 checks reuse the actual V312/V315 engines and compare queue
counts, learning, provenance, replay, stale writes, mismatches, explicit choices,
financial/source/catalog parity and client-role denials. Browser checks consume
those native payloads in the immutable V312 release at three viewport sizes.
Only synthetic records are committed or included in CI artifacts.
