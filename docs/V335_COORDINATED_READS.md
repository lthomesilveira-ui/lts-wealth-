# Coordinate guarded financial reads

An initial private-session load recovered after a retry, but classification and
wealth reads had competed with the existing expensive-reader queue. Income and
card review were missing from its read names. Wealth, awards, product and cockpit
were recognized reads but were not admitted with other costly readers, even
though they can prepare the same guarded card state.

V335 includes those queries in the existing admission mechanism, with review
and detail queries at its existing highest priority. It shares authenticated
duplicate reads, preserves response ownership and mutation invalidation, and
keeps cash and connection status independent. Queue and execution ceilings are
unchanged. Writes remain immediate, uncached and undeduplicated. There is no
database, financial source, timeout, grant, auth or paid-resource change.

The deterministic transport fixture exercises nine expensive reader kinds,
one-at-a-time admission, independent cash/status, session isolation, duplicate
classification reads, canceled queued work, mutation passthrough, superseded
responses, timeout recovery and telemetry without authentication values. The
actual release stack also runs the shared Dashboard/Actions/Flow/clipboard checks
at desktop, 390px and 360px. Published V333 and older releases are preserved.

This controls requests within one browser runtime. It is not global admission
across sessions, a server-capacity guarantee, a physical-device certificate or
proof that every source is complete. Production private-session observations
and remaining limits are recorded separately.

The shared browser fixture aligns all received dates with its frozen business
date, waits for the actual financial sections after invalidation, and compares
label/value pairs with populated cash, total liquidity and expenses. Whitespace
and intermediate loading renders are not financial values. Missing positions
cannot satisfy the preservation check.
